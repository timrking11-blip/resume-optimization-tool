Five questions

Each question targets a heavily weighted requirement the draft proves weakly, a tool or strength the posting names that your template doesn't list yet, or a consulting engagement that still needs its details. Specifics beat adjectives: numbers, scope, outcome.

DRAFT Hover a bullet to keep, swap, drop, or polish it. Click any text to edit it, including the tagline and expertise lines.

The four tiers

Evidence is what actually happened. Derived knowledge is what the bank infers from it. Generation is what gets written from that knowledge. Feedback is what happens afterwards. Nothing flows backward without a step you can see.
